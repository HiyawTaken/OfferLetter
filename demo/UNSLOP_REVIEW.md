# OfferLetter video: unslop direction

Applied the user-requested [unslop skill](https://github.com/heydamir/unslop/blob/main/skills/unslop/SKILL.md) by Damir Sokolovsky (CC BY 4.0). The user said to keep working; no direction approval pause was needed.

Product: Helps job seekers find roles, prepare applications and review before submission.
Character: Direct, useful, human.
World: Application paperwork and the job seeker's working desk.
Moments: A set of application papers becomes an envelope; the actual form and review gate provide the evidence.
Signature: The existing envelope idea from OfferLetter's wordmark, kept in one ink color.

## Audit and fixes

- The opening headline changed to 158px without moving its separately positioned subtitle. At three seconds they overlapped. Replaced independent positions with a flowing text group and explicit gap.
- Lime chips, orbital rings and a cube field were generic AI decoration. Replaced them with matte application documents, an envelope and actual review footage.
- A numbered HUD, all-caps captions, tracked text and repeated slogans competed with the product. Removed the HUD and border, reduced metadata, and wrote concrete captions in sentence case.
- Perspective swings, repeated zoom pulses and continuous rotations added activity without explaining the workflow. Kept beat-based editing, short entrances and a finite paper/envelope action.
- Display weights and sizes varied without a common layout. Uses Geist 400/500, 96–152px display sizes, 60px footage titles and stable text groups.
- A shared coordinate plane let graphics approach text. Text and 3D now have separate, bounded regions with a minimum gap.

## Verification

The renderer checks text gaps, frame safe areas, text overflow and intersection with footage/3D at every encoded frame. Dedicated stills cover 2.94, 3.0, 3.1, 3.5 and 3.9 seconds. Final MP4 is fully decoded and audio levels measured. Music, revised narration, real footage and the 90.5-second length are retained. No employer interactions or product code changes are part of this edit.

No image generation was used. Physical objects were built in the existing 3D scene; the reference was used for composition and restraint, not copied.
